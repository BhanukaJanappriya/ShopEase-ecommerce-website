Shipping Policy

Islandwide Delivery Services

ShopEase provides comprehensive delivery coverage across all districts of Sri Lanka. We partner with premier logistics carriers to ensure that your parcels are handled carefully and delivered in optimal timeframes.

Minimum Order Thresholds:

  • Orders exceeding LKR 2000.00 qualify for FREE islandwide shipping.
  • For orders under LKR 2000.00, a standard shipping fee of LKR 350.00 applies.

Delivery timeframes vary between 2 to 5 business days depending on the destination province (Western, Central, Southern, etc.). All shipments are fully trackable using our digital delivery status board.

Express Shipping

Next Day Delivery

For urgent customer orders, we offer an express shipment program which guarantees delivery by the subsequent business day.

Conditions & Geographic Boundaries:

  • Available strictly for delivery addresses inside the Colombo District (Zones 1-15 and immediate suburbs).
  • Orders must be placed and checked out before the daily cutoff limit of 3:00 PM to qualify for next-day shipping.
  • A flat express processing fee of LKR 500.00 applies, regardless of order value.
Service Hours

Best Online Support Guarantee

We are dedicated to providing you with standard-setting support and resolution. Our agents are available on live chat and telephone lines to resolve order queries, refunds, and payment issues.

Fulfillment Hours:

  • Support operations are active daily between 8:00 AM and 11:00 PM (IST).
  • Ticket inquiries logged outside these hours are guaranteed a priority response by 9:00 AM the next day.
  • Average chat queue response time is under 2 minutes.
Returns policy

Return & Replacement Policy

If you are not completely satisfied with your purchase, ShopEase permits easy and hassle-free returns within 14 days of package reception.

Key Requirements for Free Returns:

  • Products must remain unused, unwashed, and in their original packaging with tags intact.
  • Undergarments, custom jewelry, cosmetics, and swimwear cannot be returned due to hygiene policies.
  • Return shipping is entirely FREE when scheduled via our partner courier drop-off centers.
Cashback Guarantee

30% Money Back Promotion

As part of our commitment to user loyalty, we run select promotional cashback guarantees on high-value checkouts.

Promo Conditions:

  • Applicable strictly for cart values exceeding LKR 5000.00.
  • A 30% refund of the checkout total is returned as a digital coupon store credit, usable on subsequent checkouts.
  • Store credit has a validity period of 90 days from the date of coupon issuance.
